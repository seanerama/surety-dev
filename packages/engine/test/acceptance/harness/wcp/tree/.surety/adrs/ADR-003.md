# ADR-003: Millimetres as the only stored unit; inches as a display and entry unit

## Context

Specification version 2 adds a millimetres/inches selector. Switching units must never change a measurement, the layout or the offcuts, however often the woodworker switches; a plan saved by iteration 1 (millimetres, no unit) must reopen unchanged; the selected unit must survive a reload and be stated on the cut sheet. Inches are decimal only (1 in = 25.4 mm exactly), shown to at most 3 decimals; millimetres to at most 2.

## Decision

- The plan stays in millimetres, in the iteration 1 shape under `cutPlanner.plan` (`version` 1); no migration. Lengths may be non-integer millimetres.
- The selected unit is stored on its own under `cutPlanner.unit` (`"mm"` or `"in"`; anything else or missing means `"mm"`), read and written by `public/js/store.mjs`; clearing the plan keeps it.
- A new pure module `public/js/units.mjs` (units) owns conversion: `parseLength(raw, unit)` returns millimetres (inches × 25.4, unrounded) or a refusal (fractions and text such as `31in` refused, asking for a decimal number of inches); `formatLength(mm, unit)` / `formatNumber(mm, unit)` round for display only (3 decimals in inches, 2 in mm, trailing zeros dropped).
- Placement and kerf arithmetic (ADR-002) run in millimetres only. Unplaced reasons and move refusals keep their lengths in mm and are formatted into sentences in the selected unit, each figure converted from its own mm value (so R3.6's 94.724 in = 94.488 in + 0.236 in come from 2406, 2400 and 6 mm).
- An input field shows `formatNumber(stored, unit)`; the plan is updated only when the woodworker edits the field, so an unedited field keeps its stored value whatever its rounded display. Switching unit re-renders; it never writes the plan.

## Consequences

- Switching back and forth is lossless: R1.6 shows 2400 mm after any number of switches because nothing is recomputed from rounded text.
- A value typed in inches and shown in millimetres may show rounding (94.5 in -> 2400.3 mm), as the specification accepts.
- `data-length-mm` attributes stay in millimetres, so checks read the same layout in both units.
