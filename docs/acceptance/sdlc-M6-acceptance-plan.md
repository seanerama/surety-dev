# Surety M6 acceptance plan

**Status:** draft 2, 2026-10-16, **adopted by Sean with BS6** (E156), written for Sean by the owner's assistant (no Builder, Verifier or Reviewer role), in the form of the M5 plan (`docs/acceptance/sdlc-M5-acceptance-plan.md`), with the M6 build spec (`docs/spec/M6-build-spec.md`, BS6). Written against **D7 draft 3**, every question decided (its closing section; E153 item 2), reconciled with D8 (D7 §17; E155 item 2). Draft 1's eight questions are decided (E156 item 2; the closing section) and applied: M505, M530, M517, M519, M535, M540 and M541 carry them. It is the inventory the M6 build's tests will be written from: each slice's Verifier splits its rows into named cases and fixes the details of section 2.6 in the seam. No test is written by this pass and no engine code is touched. This file does not claim that any row has a test or has passed.

**Basis:** D7 draft 3 (`docs/design/sdlc-design-D7-designer.md`), its corrections G1 to G15, its Appendices A and B, and its Appendix C sketch of 35 rows (M501 to M535); Sean's decisions Q1 to Q8; D6 draft 3 as built and accepted in M5; the errata E20, E40, E64, E69, E123, E135, E142 to E155; the accepted engine's seam (`packages/engine/test/acceptance/harness/SEAM.md`, to §473); the M5 report, its not-claimed list and its real-lane README, whose lessons section 2.2 and BS6 §11.4 carry.

**Rows:** `M501` to `M546`, so no row collides with M1's `M01` to `M74`, M2's `M101` to `M142`, M3's `M201` to `M241`, M4's `M301` to `M344` or M5's `M401` to `M453`. Forty-six rows: thirty-eight kernel, one sandbox, one kernel and sandbox, two project, two real, one report and one hands-on. **M501 to M535 are D7 Appendix C's** rows, each made precise into named cases; their numbers and their statements' substance are kept, and where a case moved between them or a lane changed, section 4.1 says so. **Eleven rows are added:** M536 to M539 carry obligations of D7's text no sketched row pins (each cites its D7 section); M540 (the report) and M541 (the hands-on, short, E156 Q7) are the form of every milestone's end; **M542 to M546** carry M5's known defects (E154 item 6; E156 Q1), not D7. The M1 to M5 rows are accepted and are not re-planned; where a D7 correction changes what one of their fixtures must supply, section 4.3 says which.

## 1. Sources, authority and scope

References throughout this plan:

- **D7:** draft 3. `§n` is its section; `G1` to `G15` its §16 corrections; `Q1` to `Q8` Sean's decisions (its closing section, Q5 = G12 and Q6 = G7 as it records them); `A.n` an appendix A table; `B.n` a schema of its Appendix B.
- **D6:** draft 3, as built in M5; `D6 §n`, `D6-Xnn` its statements, `K1` to `K11` its corrections, `reading n` its §12.2.
- **D1 to D4, F, TPL, BS:** as the M5 plan. **BS6:** the M6 build spec. **E:** the errata, E1 to E155. **SEAM:** the accepted seam, §n.

D7 extends D1 to D6 and changes them only by G1 to G15. Where D7 leaves a detail to the tests, the Verifier fixes it in the seam (section 2.6). The tests are the contract; a Builder never edits an acceptance test to obtain a pass and files an objection instead; Sean keeps integration and approval authority. D7 is the last prose round (its status line): a finding after this plan is a decision for Sean or a failing acceptance test (E20). **The journey rows (M536, M516, M519, M535) run through the production paths only** (BS5 §4.1 rule 20, carried): a harness that installs an approved design, an effective asset row, a binding, a readiness or a confirmed verdict by fixture would hide the very defects D7 §6.4, §7.4, §7.5 and §10.3 close, and is not a pass.

**What M6 adds, and what these rows cover.** D7 §§2 to 13 as BS6 §3 lists them: the policy, the three axes, the inventory, the Designer session, the design product and its check, the preview, assets, approval and readiness, planning, staleness, the review, no silent stalls, retrofit, pace; one design journey on the scripted backend, two real runs; and five corrections carried from M5 (E156 Q1).

**What M6 does not cover.** BS6 §3's "Not in M6": native execution of any non-web target, a browser in the engine or the checks, image generation, a component library, D5, D8, Surety's UI. Their refusals are cases where the engine can refuse (M521 (c), M523 (f), M511 (b)).

### 2.2 Safety and containment

Every sandbox, project and real row follows BS6 §4.1's rules 1 to 26. In particular: the preview's unit only under the test home's derived name (`surety-<h>-design-preview-g<n>.service`), acted on only by exact name; never the user's manager itself; nothing the Designer or the owner wrote executed by the engine, a check or a test, and no browser, emulator or device anywhere (rules 21, 22); test asset and evidence files generated with valid headers, never real images (rule 22); the relay opened only by the test's own command and closed by its case (rule 24); the first preview on a real unit started by the driver (rule 23); a journey or real step failing at once on a silent stall or with a question it raised still open (rule 25); owner acts only through recorded acts (rule 26); every home at most 56 bytes (rule 15); the 10 s clock margin on every sandbox or project wait (rule 17). Rows M510, M516, M520, M536, M519 and M535 carry instruments under those rules. The Builder's audit list for slices 40 and 42 is not empty: the preview adds a unit start, a stop and the relay's opening for a new environment; the review adds the engine's fetches through the service link.

### 2.4 How results are observed

From outside the engine, as in M5: the API (`GET …/design`, `GET …/design/bindings`, `GET …/design/export`, `GET …/spec`, `GET …/plan`, `GET …/pace`, decisions, events, work items, runs and ledger rows), the store (D7 A.3's tables and columns), git (the integration branch, `.surety/design/`, the engine's intent commits, the side refs), the filesystem (workspaces, export directories), the scripted backend's captured packages, and on the host, read only, section 2.3's reads. A scripted role's report of what it was given is read from the captured package, never from its reply. Mockups and assets are compared as bytes and digests; nothing is rendered.

### 2.5 Honest `not_exercised`

Only the real rows if they are not run. A sandbox or project row on a host without a running user manager fails. A journey that reaches its end only because a fixture installed an intermediate state is not a pass (section 1). No case is `not_exercised` for want of a browser, an emulator or a device: no row requires one (BS6 §4.1 rule 22).

The scripted Designer's turn script format; the `design_check` record's `subjects` form per reason; the inventory's fixture seed derivation and file naming within D7 §4.4's rule; the effective set's record shape and its event; the binding record's form and how a test reads it; which stage's gate an `asset_updated` fix item's candidate re-evaluates when bindings span stages; the `design_due` question's text fields (not its prose); the batch's refusal at raise when the n+1 inventory differs (the close's product recorded, nothing raised, `design_due` kept open naming the difference: never silent); the review's fetch record's shape and its redaction; the simulation frame's exact markup and the iframe's `sandbox` attribute; the hold reason names `design_pending`, `design_stale` as D7 A.2 gives them, and M543's hold reason; the per-act record's form (`owner-acts.jsonl`, `driver-acts.jsonl`; BS6 §12); the pace read's design figures' JSON shape; the hands-on script's steps; the real lane's step names (BS6 §12) and confirmation phrases; reason and error codes where D7 A.4 and A.6 propose them (E20: the Verifier pins them).
