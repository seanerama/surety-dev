Why it came out this way

The law

Momentum is conserved: the total m·v before the collision equals the total after. In an elastic collision kinetic energy is conserved too, so the carts bounce apart.

With your numbers

v₂′ = (2·m₁·v₁ + (m₂ − m₁)·v₂) / (m₁ + m₂) = (2·2·3 + (1 − 2)·0) / (2 + 1) = 12 / 3v₂′ = 4.00 m/s

Why

Cart 1 is twice as heavy as cart 2, so it hands cart 2 more speed than it had itself: cart 2 leaves at 4 m/s and cart 1 slows to 1 m/s. The total momentum stays 6 kg·m/s and the kinetic energy stays 9 J.
