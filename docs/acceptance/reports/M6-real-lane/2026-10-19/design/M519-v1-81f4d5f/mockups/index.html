Design preview

Three lab screens — momentum, friction and trajectory — each drawn in every state the interface inventory owes, at 360, 720 and 1280 px, in a light and a dark theme. Pages follow your system theme; use the switch to force one.

Light themeDark themeFollow the system Open the momentum lab

The idea

  • Lab notebook, after dark. A cool, slightly violet neutral ground (not grey), so the page feels like a place rather than a form.
  • Two colours with jobs. Indigo is you: your prediction, the selected tab, the current step, focus. Orange is the simulation: the Run button, the moving objects, the simulated result. Green marks your guess inside the 3D scene (flag, ring) and a good score.
  • A rounded display face (system ui-rounded where the device has it, falling back to the system sans) for headings, the verdict and the big score; the system sans for reading; monospace figures for every number, so values line up and don’t jiggle while they change.
  • Playful in the words, calm in the layout. Verdicts (“Bullseye!”, “Close!”), the tab glyphs and the bouncy Run button carry the fun; the grid stays quiet.

Asset slots
