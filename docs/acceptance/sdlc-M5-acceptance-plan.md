## 1. Sources, authority and scope

References throughout this plan:

- **D6:** draft 2. `§n` is its section; `K1` to `K11` its §12.1 corrections; `Q1` to `Q9` its decided questions (E139); `N1` to `N4` the numerics (E140); `D6-Xnn` a statement of its Appendix C; `reading n` one of §12.2's twelve; `B.n` a schema of its Appendix B.
- **D1 to D4, F, TPL, SAP, IBT, BS, RN:** as the M4 plan and D6's conventions; D2's `K1` to `K10` (not D6's) are cited as `D2 Kn` where they would be confused, D3's `L1` to `L8`, D4's `J1` to `J9` stand.
- **BS5:** the M5 build spec.
- **E:** the errata, E1 to E140. **SEAM:** the accepted seam, §n.

D6 extends D1 to D4 and changes them only by K1 to K11. Where D6 leaves a detail to the tests, the Verifier fixes it in the seam (section 2.6). The tests are the contract; a Builder never edits an acceptance test to obtain a pass and files an objection instead; Sean keeps integration and approval authority. Design review of D6 stops at its approval after Astra's review: a finding after this plan is a decision for Sean or a failing acceptance test (E20).

**What M5 adds, and what these rows cover.** D6 §§2 to 8 as BS5 §3 lists them: sessions, the three roles and their products, the governed specification and its change, planning and the phase, the build's execution with its autonomy, dependencies, concurrency and tick, the pace read, retrofit; and two journeys on the scripted backend, then the two real ones.

**What M5 does not cover.** BS5 §3's "Not in M5": D5's items, several machines, adoption of a public repository, Codex and API-key sessions, provider-native continuation, the automated architecture approval, evidence reuse across a spec change, a second open session, the benchmark, the UI. Their refusals are cases where the engine can refuse (M409 (d), M419 (b), M420 (c), M423).

### 2.2 Safety and containment

Every sandbox, project, exhaust and real row follows BS5 §4.1's rules 1 to 19. In particular: units and domains only from the test's own disposable home's derived name, acted on only by exact name; never the user's manager itself; never Docker or the system manager; the Builder's audit list (expected empty in most slices) which the Reviewer checks; the runner's before-and-after reads around every run; E64's two halves for every destructive instrument (a fixture role allocating to its bound, exhaust only; any step that stops or restarts a unit in a journey's Alpha), released only after the host-side read shows the domain carries the test home's prefix; the destructive file last in its slice; no exhaustion here (rule 10: the over-limit case of M438 is the exhaust lane's); no session on a real model outside the real lane (rule 11); every home at most 56 bytes (rule 15); `TMPDIR` on disk on `mini-hp01` (rule 16); the 10 s clock margin on every sandbox or project wait and monotonic durations (rule 17); the rehearsal fakes play from the package (rule 18); nothing a session writes is run (rule 14). Rows M401, M408, M415, M420, M438 and M439 carry instruments under those rules.

### 2.3 Harness additions this plan assumes

These reach production code as ordinary parameters or seam hooks under SEAM §7's confinement rule and M74's lint, only through `src/testing/`; none is selectable outside harness mode. The Verifier names them exactly in the seam; the Builder may object.

- **The scripted backend as a conversational role** (kernel and sandbox): turn replies keyed by role and ordinal; the scripted product written into the workspace at a turn; the package captured per turn and per one-shot (so a test reads the transcript, the engine's notes and the artifact state the role was given); replies scripted to carry a `speaker` or `turn` field, `done`, `questions_for_owner`, a reply past `turn_max_bytes`, a changed requirement text, `deferred_criteria`, `adopted_tests`, `spec_change_requests`, a `requirement_conflict` finding.

- **Barriers:** `session.turn_dispatched`, `session.turn_accepted`, `session.before_save`, `plan.committed` (before its finalizer), `spec_change.approved` (before invalidation), `scheduler.tick_requested`, `dispatch.before_admission`, `check_authoring.before_proposal`, `phase.before_completion`.
- **Faults:** a turn's domain ended `infra_error`; a reply that does not parse; a save racing a turn; two Architect commits for one phase; a blob unreadable at impact; a kill between a plan's commit and its finalizer; a restart between two observations of the pace read; a restored store missing a transcript entry; a `memory.peak` file unreadable.
- **Host-side reads** (sandbox, read only): `/proc/<pid>` of a turn's domain while it runs and the absence of any process of the domain at `open_idle`; the inventory of a turn's volatile home at its end; a domain cgroup's `memory.max`, `memory.peak`, `memory.events`; M4's unit reads for a journey's Alpha.
- **The reference projects** (`harness/journey/`, `harness/retrofit/`): as BS5 §3; the journey's idea brief, the scripted sessions' transcripts and products in TPL's and Appendix B's shapes, the scripted Builders' code per stage, the change request `cr-1.md` and the scripted Spec Writer's diff, the scripted Verifier's check proposals per phase; the adopted repository with its own `test/`, a README with claims (one false, question 6), a tag `v0.1.0`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, and no `.surety/`; the retrofit sessions' products with `Origin`, the claims table and `reconstructed` ADRs; the adopted test's copy and the `developer`-origin check.
- **The package-driven rehearsal fakes** of the real lane (M442, M443, M444; BS5 §12).

### 2.4 How results are observed

From outside the engine, as in M4: the API (`GET …/sessions/:r`, `GET …/spec`, `GET …/plan`, `GET …/pace`, the project read, decisions, events, work items, runs and their ledger rows), the store (D6 A.3's tables and columns), git (the integration branch, `refs/surety/spec-change/<seq>`, the checkpoint commits), the filesystem (workspaces, the engine home, the run directory), the scripted backend's captured packages and call counts, and on the host, read only, section 2.3's reads. A scripted role's report of what it was given is read from the captured package, which the harness writes, never from the role's reply. Deterministic barriers, the controlled clock and crash-and-reopen as M1 to M4.

### 2.5 Honest `not_exercised`

Only M438's exhaust file off the designated host, and the real rows if Sean does not run them. A sandbox or project row on a host without a running user manager fails. A project-lane journey whose envelope admits one domain at a time passes on the serial path and records it in its pace read; the pipelined path is M437's and M438's to pin, not the journey's.

### 2.6 Details D6 leaves to the tests, which the Verifier fixes in the seam

The scripted backend's turn script format and how a test reads a captured package; the transcript record's `text_ref` form and how a test reads it; the engine's entry text for a parse finding (its fields, not its prose); the `plan_check` record's `subjects` form per reason; the `spec_impact` record's shape; the form of `adopted_tests` on a proposal; how a test drives a save racing a turn; how a test scripts `MemAvailable` and observes `resource_envelope` holds; the pace read's JSON shape and its `unknown` marker; how a test drives an engine restart between observations; the hands-on script's steps; the real lane's step names and confirmation phrase (BS5 §12); the adopted fixture's claims and which is false.
