## Questions for the Verifier

1. **Whose rule is broken?** Under E36 item 4 and E34 item 7 a fix completes in the gate evaluation that resolves its finding, and a finding is resolved when its check passes in an evaluation that read it. Say, with the design text, what should read a finding whose `candidate` is null (raised by a check-correction run against the project, not a candidate): the next evaluation of any candidate that runs its check; the evaluation of the fix's own candidate; or nothing, in which case a `fix` disposition on such a finding must be refused or must raise an owner question (D3 §2.10's form, rule 25: no silent hold). Errata E36 item 6 says "a fix that names no finding, or whose finding names no check, can never complete"; this finding names both, and still cannot complete. Decide whether that is a gap in the rule or in the engine.
2. **What happens to a plain `fix` held by a superseded candidate?** `finalize.ts` completes a **stage** item superseded while running (`end_cause` superseded) and the due-gates loop carries `asset_updated` fixes by ancestry; a plain fix held by a superseded candidate is in neither path. Say what the design requires: carried to the successor candidate's held work and judged at its stage gate there, or returned to eligible, or completed superseded with its finding left open and an owner question raised. "Without silently dropping work" (Sean): a resolution that marks the fix complete while its finding stays unresolved and nothing asks about it is a drop.
3. **Should the harness count a `verifying` item with no run, no due gate and no pending verification as runnable?** `activity().runnable` is rule 25's "runnable" set. Decide whether `verifying` with nothing the engine can dispatch is "in flight" (the harness waits for ever), "runnable" (the same), or neither (nothing runnable, nothing in flight, nothing asked: a silent stall the harness must fail at once, naming the item). The last reading matches rule 25 as written and E162 item 1; it changes the step's verdict from a deadline failure to an immediate stall failure naming the fix, which is the honest outcome. It must not be made to let the step pass.
4. **Failing cases first.** Build, through the engine's API in the kernel lane (no browser, no real model, no real lane), at least: (a) a `fix` disposition on a finding with no candidate binding, its Builder's candidate nominated and verified, the next stage gate passing its check, and the assertion that the finding resolves and the fix completes, or that the engine raises a question naming the fix (whichever question 1 decides); (b) a plain fix held by a candidate that is then superseded, with the assertion from question 2; (c) the harness's `activity()` on a store in the observed state (a `verifying` fix with no run, no due gate, no pending verification) asserting what question 3 decides, in M512 or the file that owns the harness's rule-25 cases. Name the row each case belongs to.
5. **The proposed correction, within role boundaries.** A harness correction (question 3) is the Verifier's and may be made on this branch after its failing case. An engine correction (questions 1 and 2) is the Builder's: describe it precisely in the answer file (the rule, the transition, the event, the question text if any) and leave `packages/engine/src/` unchanged. Do not edit the run store. Do not weaken any acceptance rule, assertion or row to obtain a pass, and do not let any correction make M535's review step pass when it would not have: with the fix settled, M535 (l) still fails on the driver's performer record, as it should.
